# Brand direction

Status: **draft for owner review** (2026-10-24). Source: `docs/brief.md` §3, §4 and §15.
Companion files: `docs/design/system.md` (design system), `docs/design/tokens.css` (tokens), `docs/design/mark.svg` (placeholder mark).

Nothing in this file states facts about the organisation. The brand name, venue, people, prices, legal status and accreditation all come from configuration supplied by the owner.

---

## 1. Positioning

A small, calm learning community for children in grades 1–4 (about ages 6–10), for families around Banská Bystrica, Ružomberok and Liptovský Mikuláš. Children learn by discovering, handling real materials, talking things through and spending time outdoors. The community draws on Montessori principles, respectful communication, constructivist learning and the Hejný approach to mathematics. It is for thoughtful parents who want their child to understand rather than memorise, and who still expect clear goals, structure and honest feedback on progress. The brand never defines itself against conventional schools. It describes what happens here, concretely, and lets parents judge the fit.

**One-line test.** After the hero, a parent should be able to say: *"It's a small community for grades 1–4 near us. Children learn by discovering things at their own pace, and I can enrol or come to meet them."*

**Brand idea: a clearing (svetlina).** A clearing is an open, light place within the forest: protected but not closed, a place to look around, rest and then explore further. It ties together nature, calm, room to grow and seeing clearly (understanding). The idea drives the mark, the organic frames and the landscape illustration. It works without a name, so it survives whichever name the owner chooses.

## 2. Voice and tone

**Voice (constant):** warm, calm, confident, intelligent, accessible. We speak to parents as equals.

**Rules**

1. **Concrete before abstract.** Every principle is followed by an example from a real school day ("children measure the classroom with their own steps before they use a metre").
2. **Plain Slovak.** Explain a term the first time it appears (Hejného matematika, konštruktivizmus). No academic jargon or anglicisms where a Slovak word exists.
3. **Honest about uncertainty.** Unknown operational facts say so: *"čaká na potvrdenie"*, *"ilustračný príklad"*. Never round an intention up to a promise.
4. **No comparison by contempt.** We describe our approach; we never say traditional schools "kill curiosity".
5. **No guarantees.** No "every child will…", "guaranteed results", "exceptional children".
6. **Calm CTAs.** Invitations, not pressure: no countdowns, "last places" or urgency we cannot prove.
7. **Accurate labels.** "inspirovaný Montessori" (not "Montessori škola"); "pracujeme s prístupom Hejného matematiky" (no certification claimed); the legal status is named exactly as configured.
8. **Address parents with *vy*** (formal, warm plural). Children are "deti", never "žiaci" in marketing copy, unless the legal context requires it.
9. **Short sentences, active verbs.** One idea per sentence in leads; paragraphs of at most 3–4 sentences on mobile.
10. **Inclusive by default.** We describe what the community offers, not who is unwelcome (see §2.2).

### 2.2 Tone by context

| Context | Tone | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Hero, philosophy | warm, evocative, but specific | one image, one promise of experience, no superlatives |
| Approach pages | explanatory, patient | definition → practice → example → benefit → what parents can expect |

## 4. Logo direction

**Concept: the clearing.** An organic pebble shape in forest green with an opening (cream) where a meadow (sage) meets a small sun (yellow). It reads as a landscape seen through an opening, and as a stone you can hold, a Montessori-like natural material. See `docs/design/mark.svg` (placeholder; paths only, no text, no `foreignObject`, no SMIL).

**Lock-ups**
- *Horizontal (default):* mark at 36 px + wordmark in Fraunces 560, `--step-1`, `--color-heading`, gap `--space-2xs`. Used in the header.
- *Stacked:* mark 64 px above the wordmark, centred. Used in the footer, social cards and print.
- *Mark only:* favicon, app icon, small social avatars. Minimum size 24 px; below that, drop the meadow shape (favicon variant to be drawn at 16/32 px).

**Rules**
- Clear space: at least the height of the sun dot × 2 on every side.
- On forest or night surfaces: the pebble becomes cream, the opening forest, the meadow sage and the sun stays yellow (inverse variant to be drawn).
- Do not rotate, outline, add gradients or shadows, or set the wordmark in another font.
- The final logo should be drawn by hand (pen-and-paper roughness, slightly irregular curves) once the name is chosen. The placeholder shows only the geometry and the colour logic.

## 5. Imagery and illustration

### 5.1 Photography (preferred, when authentic)

- **Authentic first.** Real photos of the community's own space, materials and outdoor time, taken with written parental consent recorded per image (GDPR; the consent record lives outside the CMS image file).
- **Children:** from behind, hands at work, small figures in a landscape, or faces only with documented consent. Never the sole visual of a child's face in the hero without explicit consent for that use.
- **Subjects:** hands with materials (wood, clay, paper, textiles), mathematical manipulatives, books and drawings, plants, outdoor learning, natural light. No staged "thumbs-up" classrooms.
- **Treatment:** warm white balance, natural light, soft contrast and no heavy filters. Crop to the organic frame (`--radius-organic`) or the pebble frame (`--radius-pebble`). Rectangular full-bleed only for landscape bands.
- **Licensed stock** is allowed only for non-people subjects (materials, landscapes, nature details) and must be labelled in the CMS with the licence and source. Stock images of children must never be presented as this community's pupils. Use them only when unavoidable, captioned as illustrative.
- **Never AI-generated children or staged scenes presented as real students** (brief §4). AI imagery of people is not used at all. AI-assisted non-photographic artwork, if ever used, is labelled as illustration.
- **Every photo:** explicit `width` and `height`, AVIF/WebP with a JPEG fallback via `<picture>`, responsive `srcset`, `loading="lazy"` except the hero image, meaningful `alt` in each language (or `alt=""` when decorative), and a caption when it adds context.
- **Until real photos exist:** use illustrations (below) or a clearly marked placeholder frame: sage-100 field, mark silhouette, caption „Ilustrácia – nahradí ju fotografia z našej komunity“.

### 5.2 Illustration

- **Style:** flat organic shapes cut like paper, with slightly irregular edges and a few hand-drawn lines. Palette tokens only (`--color-deco-*`, forest). At most 3–4 colours per illustration, generous negative space, no outlines around filled shapes, no gradients.
- **Subjects:** layered Slovak hills (Low/High Tatra silhouettes stylised, not maps), stones, leaves, wooden blocks, paper, the sun, footpaths.
- **Hejný-style mathematical motifs, used tastefully** as quiet line drawings in forest or clay at 1.5–3 px stroke, like a teacher's sketch in a notebook:
  - sum triangles (*súčtové trojuholníky*), circles joined by lines;
  - stepping paths (*krokovanie*) as dotted footpaths;
  - stairs (*schody*) as stepped blocks;
  - cobwebs (*pavučiny*) as node-and-edge diagrams;
  - parquet tilings (*parkety*) as polyomino patterns;
  - sticks (*drievka*) as arranged lines.
  Use them as section ornaments, pillar motifs and backgrounds at low visual weight. Never put characters, faces or mascots on them.
- **Not childish:** no cartoon children, smiling suns, rainbows, crayon fonts, primary colours or clip-art.
- **Regional cue:** the hero landscape may suggest hills and a path between three areas but **must not show location pins or town markers implying a venue in each city** (brief §2: no fictional operating locations).
- **Delivery:** SVG, decorative ones with `alt=""` / `aria-hidden="true"` and no `<title>`; informative ones with a localised text alternative. No `foreignObject`, no SMIL, no external references, no inline `style` attributes (CSP); use presentation attributes. Target under 6 KB per ornament and 20 KB for the hero landscape.
- **Texture (optional):** a 2–4 KB tiled paper-grain WebP at 3–5 % opacity on cream bands only. Never on text containers that must keep contrast; never as a CSS filter.

## 6. Motion principles

Motion is **natural, quiet and purposeful**: things settle like a page being laid down, not like an app showing off. Tokens: `--duration-*`, `--ease-*` and `--reveal-distance` in `tokens.css`.

1. **Feedback first.** Hover, press and focus respond within `--duration-instant`/`--duration-fast` (colour, underline thickness, a 1–2 px nudge of an arrow). No scale bounces.
2. **Disclosure.** Accordions, the mobile nav and FAQ answers open over `--duration-base` with `--ease-out`. Height animation is progressive (`interpolate-size: allow-keywords` where supported; otherwise instant).
3. **Reveal on scroll is optional and rare.** At most one reveal per section (heading block or illustration), opacity 0→1 plus at most `--reveal-distance` (12 px) of travel, `--duration-slow`, `--ease-organic`. Pure CSS (`animation-timeline: view()` inside `@supports`). Content is visible by default if the feature or JS is missing, so nothing is ever hidden behind an animation.
4. **Page transitions.** Same-origin cross-document View Transitions (`@view-transition { navigation: auto; }`) with a short cross-fade, including the language switch. CSS only, CSP-safe.
5. **Never:** parallax, autoplaying carousels, looping background motion, animated counters, scroll-jacking, or anything that moves for more than 5 s (WCAG 2.2.2).
6. **Reduced motion (`prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`):** the tokens collapse durations to 0 and travel to 0. Reveals render in their final state, View Transitions are disabled (`@view-transition { navigation: none; }` inside the media query), and the accordion opens instantly. Colour and focus changes stay; they are not motion.
7. Animate only `opacity` and `transform` (and `background-color` on controls); never layout properties, so motion causes no CLS.

## 7. Avoid list (brief §15 plus brand specifics)

- Generic stock-template design: centred hero with a stock photo of smiling children and two pill buttons, with nothing else distinctive.
- Corporate gradients, glassmorphism, neon accents, dark "SaaS" heroes.
- Excessive cards: no grids of shadowed boxes for pillars, values or skills. Use editorial rows, lists, columns and hairlines (system.md §6.4).
- Large generic icon grids (8 icons + 8 labels for "what children develop"). Use typographic lists with one illustration at most.
- Overwhelming animation: parallax, bouncing, autoplay sliders, confetti.
- Artificial testimonials, invented quotes, stock-photo "parents", fake ratings or logos.
- Empty inspirational statements ("Vzdelávanie budúcnosti", "Kde sa rodia hviezdy"). Each claim needs an example.
- Inconsistent typography: only Fraunces and Atkinson Hyperlegible Next, only the tokenised scale, no faux bold or italic.
- Overly childish illustrations: mascots, cartoon children, crayon fonts, rainbow palettes.
- Urgency and scarcity ("posledné miesta"), unless confirmed and true.
- Claims of accreditation, certification, school status or guaranteed outcomes without verified facts.
- Location pins or "pobočky" in Banská Bystrica, Ružomberok and Liptovský Mikuláš.
- Text on photographs without a solid scrim that guarantees AA contrast.
- Terracotta or yellow text on cream (fails AA: 3.06 and 1.49).
